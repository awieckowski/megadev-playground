0002 · Duplicate reports share an established cause

owner awieckowski with Codex proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Proposed replacement for: 0001 · Duplicates share a subject line

Context

The #4471 review shows that subject-only merging combined two date problems. The existing interactive triage component already compares causes, not subjects. Its prose and accepted decision disagree with what runs.

Proposed decision

Merge only when evidence establishes the same cause. Look up the tenant’s locale and booking-form version, preserve the report’s channel, and apply the supported classifier. Subjects and intake tags may help find candidates but cannot establish duplication.

Unknown tenants or unclassified reports require investigation. “Both unknown” does not mean “same cause.” Keep separate tickets for the parser and booking-v3 hint problems. Count distinct report IDs; preserve receipt and merge timestamps separately.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and checking tenant settings. This costs more than matching a subject but avoids hiding a separate bug behind a known ticket. Add new evidence to the appropriate issue and review the classifier when a new product, form, or failure mode appears; do not generalize this two-cause classifier to all support incidents.

Decision 0001 is preserved byte-for-byte. This record is proposed, not evidence of a production policy change. If accepted, publish its status and update the active triage guide before applying it. Accepted decision records remain immutable.
